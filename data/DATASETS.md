# Dataset inputs

The full panel uses five stratified 70/30 splits with seeds
1907, 2027, 4049, 6029 and 8089. Feature matrices are obtained from
the source datasets and are not included in this release.
Dataset versions and reuse terms are in full-panel.json; exact split
hashes are in full-study-processed/manifest.json.
